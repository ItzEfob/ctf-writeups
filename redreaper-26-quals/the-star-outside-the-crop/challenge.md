# The Star Outside the Crop

**Category** Satellite OSINT  
**Difficulty** Hard

![The Star Outside the Crop cover](monkey.jpeg)

The monkey who runs the night projection booth marked a double star on a paired photograph. In the ground-based frame the stars blur together. In the orbital frame the two points separate cleanly. He swears the marked pair was the spacecraft's pointing target.

The filing card also has two dates and a pencilled exposure length. Identify the comparison and restore the five fields before the monkey files it again.

## Your task

Find the archived comparison made from the space telescope's initial focusing test. The left panel is a ground-based photograph from Chile. The right is a crop of the same field taken in orbit. Start from [NASA's public Hubble image gallery](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/multimedia/hubble-images/) and reconcile the image catalog, mission chronology, and account of the exposure.

Submit these five fields in order.

1. The ESA/Hubble media catalog's **Id** for the two-panel comparison.
2. The day NASA's mission timeline records its first image being taken.
3. The date shown in the ESA/Hubble catalog entry's **Release date** field for the comparison.
4. The star catalog designation at the center of the full orbital exposure. The crop can exclude that star.
5. The duration in seconds of the longer orbital exposure used for the published comparison.

Format the answer as `rxr{monkey_mediaid_acquireddate_catalogdate_target_seconds}`. Lowercase the identifiers, remove spaces from the target designation, use `yyyymmdd` for both dates, and give the duration as a whole number with no unit suffix. The clues are fully available through a browser.
